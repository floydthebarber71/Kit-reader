Kit Reader

Random drum-kit reading lines, graded from Grade 3 up to Grade 6. Read the line, play it to the click, then listen to how it should sound.

Time signatures

Each new line uses one of the ticked time signatures, chosen at random. Leave only 4/4 ticked for the standard lines, or tick a single one to practise it on its own. In 6/8 the tempo counts dotted quarter notes, so there are two beats in a bar.

How to practise with it
  1. Press Generate new line. Look at it for ten seconds and count it out loud, without playing.
  2. Press Play. After the count-in, play the line on your kit along with the click, and keep going through mistakes. In the exam there is no stopping. Press Play again to pause, and Stop to go back to the start.
  3. Press Hear kit at any point to switch the kit sound on while the line keeps playing, and again to switch it off. Tick "Click while kit is on" if you want the click under the kit.
  4. Start at a tempo where you play it cleanly first time, and raise it a little each week. Tap the tempo number to type an exact value.

The levels are my own rough fit to the Grade 3 to 6 range. They are not official RSL material, so compare them with the sample sight-reading tests on the RSL website. Hi-hat is the x above the staff, snare is the middle space, kick is the bottom space. Notes in brackets are ghost notes (play them very quietly).
